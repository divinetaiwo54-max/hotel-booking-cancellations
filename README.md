# Hotel Booking Cancellations Analysis (Python)

Why do 37% of hotel bookings get cancelled, and how much revenue does it cost?

Analysis of 119,000 bookings from two hotels in Portugal (2015–2017) using pandas, matplotlib and seaborn.

## Key findings
- **37.1%** of bookings are cancelled, putting **€13.1M** of revenue at risk.
- Bookings made over a year ahead cancel **67.7%** of the time vs **9.6%** within a week.
- Direct bookings cancel **15%**, compared with **61%** for groups.
- Non-refundable deposits show 99% cancellation, but 97% of these come from groups and agents, not individual guests.
- Engaged guests (repeat visitors, special requests) cancel far less.

## Recommendations
Deposits on long-lead bookings, incentives for direct booking, tighter group contracts, and pre-arrival engagement emails.
